# BOO! — The Haunted Collection

**Tiny Haunts. Legendary Stories.**

A free, mobile-friendly digital booster-pack opening and collecting prototype, with retro Halloween artwork and mischievous ghosts inspired by real haunted places.

## Play it online (GitHub Pages)

1. Open this repository's **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
4. Once GitHub finishes publishing, visit **https://glitchedduck.github.io/boo-haunted-collection/**.

The entire app lives in **index.html**, including its images, so no build step or external dependencies are required.

On iPhone, open the live site in Safari and use **Share → Add to Home Screen** if you want a shortcut.

## What's included

- Tap or swipe to rip open Midnight Manor booster packs
- Six cards per pack: 3 Common, 2 Uncommon and 1 Rare or better
- Proposed rare-slot odds: 89% Rare, 10% Ultra Rare and 1% Secret Rare
- 36 cards to collect: 20 Ghosts, 8 Tricks, 8 Haunted Locations
- Card reveals, special rare effects and a collection binder
- Original BOO! characters and short real-world haunting inspirations
- Automatic local collection saving in your browser
- Free play — **no payments, logins, trading or real-world prizes**

## Important notes

This is a **pack-opening/collection prototype**, not a finished competitive TCG. The odds are prototype values generated with client-side randomness; they are not independently audited. Cards have no cash value, and collections are not transferable between devices. Browser data clearing may reset your progress. Folklore and ghost sightings are presented as legends rather than established historical facts.

## Run locally

Download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser. Everything is bundled into the one file.
